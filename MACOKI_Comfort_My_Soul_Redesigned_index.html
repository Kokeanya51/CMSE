ABOUT MACOKI

One connected home for essential services.

A digital platform created to bring healthcare, education, patient services, professional services, school learning, pharmacy and inventory management, and maternal & reproductive health information together.

MACOKI Clinic and Maternity Home

MACOKI Clinic and Maternity Home is the healthcare foundation of this ecosystem, designed around accessible, compassionate, preventive, maternal, child, community and general healthcare while using appropriate digital systems to improve communication, coordination and continuity of care.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To build a trusted, accessible and technology-enabled healthcare and education ecosystem that improves quality, accessibility, efficiency and continuity of essential services.

Mission: To provide compassionate, accessible and responsible healthcare and educational services while using appropriate technology to connect people with information, professionals and services.

❤️CompassionDignity, empathy and respect.
✓IntegrityHonesty, transparency and accountability.
♿AccessibilityUseful services and information, easier to reach.
🔒PrivacyPersonal and health information handled with care.
